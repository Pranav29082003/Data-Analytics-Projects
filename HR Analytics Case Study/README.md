<h1>HR Analytics Dashboard - Employee Attrition Analysis</h1> 


<h3>Project Overview</h3>
This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition data to identify workforce trends, understand factors influencing employee turnover, and provide actionable insights for improving employee retention.

Using SQL and Power BI, I performed exploratory data analysis, calculated key HR metrics, and developed an interactive dashboard to help HR teams make data-driven decisions.

<h3>Business Problem</h3>
Employee attrition can significantly impact organizational productivity, recruitment costs, and overall business performance.
The objective of this project is to:
  •	Measure employee attrition rates.
  •	Identify employee groups with high turnover.
  •	Analyze factors contributing to attrition.
  •	Provide insights to support retention strategies.

<h3>Dataset</h3>

Dataset Used: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset

<h3>Key Insights</h3>
•	Overall employee attrition rate is approximately 16%.
•	Employee turnover varies significantly across departments.
•	Overtime is strongly associated with higher attrition.
•	Lower salary groups exhibit higher turnover rates.
•	Employee satisfaction and work-life balance influence retention.
•	Early-career employees show increased attrition risk.
